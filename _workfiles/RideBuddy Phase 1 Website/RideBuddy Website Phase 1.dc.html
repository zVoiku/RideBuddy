What RideBuddy is

RideBuddy will connect you with a Buddy: a verified, trained driver who drives your own car on outstation trips from Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula. Round trips to the hills and back, or one way. You'll keep your car, your seats and your music. Your Buddy will handle the road.
