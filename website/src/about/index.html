What RideBuddy is

Questions, answered.

When does booking open?

Soon. Join the waitlist and you’ll get one message when it does.

What happens after I join the waitlist?

Nothing until booking opens. Then one message, by SMS or WhatsApp. Nothing else.

Is this a cab service?

No. You travel in your own car. RideBuddy will provide the driver, called a Buddy.

Who are the Buddies?

Every Buddy is verified and trained before their first trip, and rated by you after every trip.

Is the estimate the final price?

It’s a specific estimate for your exact trip. The final fare is calculated when your trip ends, from the actual trip.

What about tolls and parking?

Tolls and parking aren’t part of the fare. You pay them directly, as you would on any trip in your own car.

What does my Buddy’s stay cost on multi-day trips?

Your estimate already includes it. The stay cost will be shown in your price breakdown when booking opens. Arranging the stay yourself? Choose Yes in the estimator and it’s removed. Many hotels give your Buddy a room at no cost.

Where can I travel?

Pickups across the Chandigarh Tricity: Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula.
