Built on one promise.

Safety and reliability, for every family, on every road.

More families in the Tricity own a car than ever before. Finding someone to drive it has not kept pace. The market that fills the gap runs on referrals and phone numbers passed between neighbours, and every fare is a negotiation.

Your car is personal space. Whoever sits in the driver's seat earns that place through verification and training, not through a recommendation you had no way to check.

RideBuddy exists to make that check the default. Every Buddy is police-verified, formally trained, and rated after every trip. You see the fare before you commit, and you travel in your own car.
