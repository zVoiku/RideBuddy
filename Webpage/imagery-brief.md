# RideBuddy Website — Imagery Brief

Three photo surfaces on the site. Each is a drop-in slot: drag an image file onto it in the
preview and it stays. Nothing else needs changing in the build.

Source of truth: Brand Kit Section 4 (Photography & Imagery Guidelines).
§4.4 in the handoff lists the prompt titles only — the prompts below are written from the
locked §4.1 decision table. **Not founder-approved wording — flag if §4.4's originals differ.**

---

## Locked decisions these prompts encode (§4.1)

| Decision | Choice |
|---|---|
| Emotional anchor | Freedom & openness |
| Primary subject | The Buddy — trust in a human face, not a road or car |
| Buddy style | Smart casual, dark navy polo + RideBuddy badge |
| Setting | The customer's own car, parked or on road |
| Light | Golden hour, early morning |
| Customer presence | Buddy solo (default) · Buddy + family (outstation campaigns) |
| Family | North Indian parents + 1–2 kids, candid, unposed |
| Grade | Warm golden — greens and ambers |

**Banned in every frame (§4.3):** teal/cyan grade · generic stock feel · staged poses ·
low-light or gritty urban · competitor vehicles or branding · traffic-stress imagery ·
subservient Buddy posture.

**DALL·E 3**

> A cinematic wide travel photograph taken at early golden hour on a Himachal mountain road.
> An Indian man in his early thirties, wearing a dark navy polo shirt with a small embroidered

## Checklist before a shot goes live (§4.5)

- Warm golden grade — greens and ambers, zero teal or cool blue
- The Buddy reads as capable and equal, never deferential
- The car is plausibly the customer's own, no competitor branding visible
- Candid, not posed; no forced smiles
- Daylight, open road or driveway — no traffic, no congestion, no gritty urban
- Hero under 250KB as WebP on mobile; in-app photos under 200KB
